📋 Changelog

What's changed on this site, and when each course section's source file was last prepared.

Site changes

2026-07-30

Published 7 previously unreleased Blender animations (Visual Aids now 24 total). These had been built and rendered earlier but were never wired into the site:

  • The Three "Killers" — airway obstruction, bleeding, and shock (Section 3, slide 6)
  • How to Approach a Survivor — be seen, identify, consent, respect (Section 3, slide 7). These two give Section 3 its first visual aids.
  • CERT Size-Up — The 9-Step Model — drawn as a loop rather than a list, because the course stresses that size-up never actually finishes (Section 5, slide 8)
  • AVPU, SAMPLE, OPQRST, and the Glasgow Coma Scale — patient-assessment mnemonics

The last four are not part of CERT Basic Training — they appear nowhere in the course decks. They're filed under a separate "Patient Assessment · Supplementary Reference" heading rather than presented as course content, and each states in its description that it's beyond CERT scope of practice. They're included so CERT members can understand terminology they'll hear professional responders use; the Glasgow Coma Scale in particular is a clinical tool, and CERT's own equivalents are the simpler AVPU check or the mental-status step inside START triage.

2026-07-29

Added 4 new Visual Aids (now 17 total) covering recently added material that had no diagram yet:

  • Burn Classifications — superficial, partial thickness, and full thickness, tied to its actual course slide (Section 4, slide 25), which also gives Section 4 its first inline transcript diagram
  • Hazmat Control Zones — the hot, warm, and cold zones, and which one CERT works in
  • Watch vs. Warning — the National Weather Service distinction between "get ready" and "act now"
  • ICS & NIMS Training Pathway — the IS-100 → IS-200 → IS-700 → IS-800 free course progression

A fifth planned diagram — the P.A.S.S. fire-extinguisher technique — was not shipped. Across three rounds and twelve candidates the design generator never produced a correct version: it repeatedly merged the "AIM" and "SQUEEZE" steps into one, dropped instruction lines, or attached the wrong instruction to a step. Publishing a fire-extinguisher procedure with steps in the wrong order wasn't worth the risk, so it was left out rather than shipped imperfect.

2026-07-29

Added 6 more links to Field Reference (now 51 total), rounding out the ICS/NIMS training set and the volunteer ecosystem CERT operates within:

  • Added IS-200.c (Basic ICS for Initial Response) and IS-800.d (National Response Framework) to complete the IS-100/200/700/800 NIMS course progression
  • Added IS-317.a: Introduction to CERT — the free primer often used as a prerequisite before CERT Basic Training, distinct from IS-315's focus on ICS
  • Added FEMA's official national CERT program page and the Medical Reserve Corps (a common volunteer partner alongside CERT) to Incident Command & Coordination
  • Added FEMA's Office of Disability Integration and Coordination (access & functional needs guidance) to Family & Community Preparedness
  • Every new URL checked live before shipping, including confirming a redirect (HHS's old MRC URL now resolves through ASPR) and linking the resolved address directly
2026-07-29

Closed the last two Field Reference coverage gaps (now 45 links, 9 categories) — every one of the 8 course sections now has at least one link:

  • Light Search & Rescue (Section 5) had zero links — added a new Search & Rescue category with FEMA's Urban Search & Rescue (US&R) Response System (the professional "heavy" S&R system CERT's light search and rescue feeds into) and OSHA's Structural Collapse Guide
  • Disaster Medical Ops — Part 2 (Section 4) had zero links — added CDC guidance on food and water safety, water/sanitation/hygiene (WASH), and post-disaster injury prevention
  • Every new URL was checked live before shipping; two candidates were rejected after verification — a 2008 FEMA marking-system PDF (triggers a download, can't confirm content) and CDC's old wound-care page (404s, replaced with CDC's current consolidated post-disaster-injury page)
